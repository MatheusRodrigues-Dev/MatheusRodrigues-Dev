### 🏢 Work at Smart Sensor Design

At **Smart Sensor Design**, I work on the development, improvement, and support of software solutions connected to real-world products, internal processes, and IoT-based systems.

Some of the projects and areas I have been involved with include:

- ⏱️ **Smart Time Work**  
  Development and support of a work-time management application, focused on organizing employee time records, schedules, and operational routines.

- 💧 **Smart Flow Hall**  
  Software and product support for an intelligent water filter monitoring solution, including app flows, dashboards, landing page content, product presentation materials, and UX improvements.

- ⚡ **Smart Energy Meter**  
  Support and development related to energy consumption monitoring solutions, involving application interfaces, data visualization, and user-centered improvements.

- 🎣 **Smart Fish Finder**  
  Participation in software and UX-related improvements for an application focused on fishing support, including information such as depth, temperature, and fish detection.

- 💰 **SSD Financial System**  
  Development of an internal financial organization system to help manage bank statements, invoices, payments, classifications, and company financial workflows.

- 🌐 **Landing Pages and Product Pages**  
  Creation and improvement of landing pages for company products, focusing on clear communication, responsive layouts, product positioning, and conversion-oriented structure.

- 🧪 **UX Research and Heuristic Evaluation**  
  Involvement in research activities related to Nielsen’s heuristics, usability analysis, UX evaluation of applications, and academic writing connected to software engineering.
